## 4. Copy patterns to USE

These are the patterns the source material rewards — Phase 2 should lean into them.

1. **First-person plural for the business voice.** "We've been the electricians..." not "SmartWire is..." — the company's own copy uses "we" consistently and it makes the family-business feel land.
2. **Owner-named credibility.** Terry Henry's name appears in nearly every customer testimonial (*"Terry Henry @ SmartWire..."*, *"Terry has great work ethics..."*, *"hired Terry for any and all electrical needs"*). The about-page should center Terry by name. The hero can name him too if Phase 2 decides — "Terry Henry's been wiring Atlanta homes since 2004" reads stronger than the abstract "SmartWire's been serving Atlanta since 2004."
3. **Specific tenure claims.** "Since 2004," "20+ years," "30+ years personal experience" — concrete years beat vague "decades."
4. **Concrete services.** "Rewiring houses," "ceiling fans," "recessed lighting," "electrical troubleshooting." Not generic "electrical solutions."
5. **Trade-credential anchoring.** Master Electrician, US Air Force trained, former IEC Atlanta instructor. These are the trust-strip + about-page differentiators.
6. **Family-feel framing.** "Atlanta homeowners feeling like a part of our family." Husband-and-wife or sibling officer team (Terry President, Maria VP). The brand voice is warm, personal, owner-operated.
7. **"Fix-when-others-can't" hero narrative.** This appears across multiple testimonials. It's a dependable-last-resort positioning, distinct from "fastest" or "cheapest." Phase 2 should consider featuring this — it's an unusual moat for residential electricians.

## 5. Copy patterns to AVOID

These show up in the source material but should NOT make it into the new site, either because they undersell, mismatch positioning, or are generic.

1. **Don't use "world class service."** It's in the KP copy but it's empty corporate language. The customer testimonials are richer; quote those.
2. **Don't lead with commercial/institutional projects.** Georgia Tech / VA Hospital / Dobbins AFB / Coca-Cola Distribution Centers are real Terry-Henry-career projects but residential homeowners (the actual ICP) don't care. Use them as a single about-page line ("Terry's worked on projects across Atlanta") at most.
3. **Don't say "since 2020"** even though the LLC was registered then. The brand lineage runs back to 2004. Use "since 2004" / "20+ years."
4. **Don't claim BBB Accreditation.** SmartWire is NOT BBB Accredited (per BBB profile). Don't put a BBB-accredited badge on the site. If Phase 2 wants a third-party-trust badge, use the GA SOS Master Electrician credential instead.
5. **Don't lead with "appliance repair."** It's mentioned in WebSearch synthesis but absent from owner-controlled surfaces (Alignable, Facebook). May be misattributed; treat as unconfirmed until Bruce verifies.
6. **Don't include the (404) 635-6301 phone.** That's the Google KP secondary line — likely Terry's direct cell. Phone CTAs across the site should use **(404) 382-9847** (the BBB / Yelp / Facebook / business-promoted number).
7. **Don't write "smartwire365.com" anywhere on the new site.** That domain is currently parked. The new site lives under `<slug>.pages.dev` (Mini's deploy convention). If SmartWire revives the domain later, that's a Mini DNS-cutover task, not site copy.
