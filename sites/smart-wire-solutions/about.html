About us

The team behind SmartWire Solutions.

Terry Henry started wiring Atlanta homes in 2004. Twenty-plus years later, he's still the one showing up to most of the jobs.

Terry's a Master Electrician. Trained in the U.S. Air Force, taught a generation of Atlanta electricians through the Independent Electrical Contractors Association. But his real specialty is the unglamorous stuff: the flickering kitchen light, the dead outlet behind the dresser, the panel that was wired wrong twenty years ago.

SmartWire is family-run. Terry's the President; his wife Maria handles operations as Vice President. We pick up our own phone, show up when we say we will, and write you a real estimate before we touch a wire. After two decades, we'd rather build a 20-year relationship with you than rush in and out.

Meet the team

Terry Henry

Terry Henry is a Master Electrician with over thirty years of experience. Trained in the U.S. Air Force, mentored a generation of Atlanta electricians as an instructor for the Independent Electrical Contractors Association, and has wired everything from kitchen circuits to large-format projects across the city. In 2004 he founded what would become SmartWire Solutions to focus on the residential work he could put his name on. His wife Maria runs operations as Vice President. The two of them have been treating Atlanta homeowners like family for over twenty years.
